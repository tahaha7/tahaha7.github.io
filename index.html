🛒 Market Basket Analysis — Retail Insights from Transaction Data

Python · pandas · matplotlib · seaborn · mlxtend (Apriori)

End-to-end market basket analysis on a real-world groceries dataset (member number, purchase date, item description). Followed the full data science lifecycle: cleaned and structured transaction data, ran exploratory analysis on frequently purchased items, grouped purchases into baskets and encoded them as a binary matrix, then applied the Apriori algorithm to mine frequent itemsets and generate high-confidence, high-lift association rules — answering "what items are commonly bought together?"

Visualized support, confidence, and lift with scatter plots. Shows how minimal transaction-level data can drive retail business intelligence and recommendation systems.
