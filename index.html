🖼️ Image Classification with a CNN

Python · PyTorch · torchvision · numpy · matplotlib

Classifies 32×32 color images into 10 everyday categories (airplane, automobile, bird, cat, deer, dog, frog, horse, ship, truck) using the real CIFAR-10 dataset (60,000 images). Trained a small CNN from scratch — two conv blocks with max-pooling and a 512-unit classifier head — with augmentation (random crops + horizontal flips), reaching 76.1% accuracy on the 10,000-image test set.

Full deep-learning pipeline: augmentation on the training split only, Adam with learning-rate step decay, per-epoch test tracking, a 10×10 confusion matrix, and per-class accuracy — ship (88.5%) and automobile (88.2%) lead, cat (58.0%) trails — with the trained weights saved for reuse.

📈 Store Sales Forecasting

Python · scikit-learn · LightGBM · pandas · matplotlib

Forecasts daily grocery sales for Corporación Favorita (Ecuador) using 4.5 years of real store data — 3M+ rows across 33 product families and 54 stores. Compared a naive baseline, Ridge, and LightGBM on a 16-day holdout with the competition metric RMSLE — LightGBM won at 0.34 (vs 0.57 Ridge, 0.64 naive).

Full time-series pipeline: leak-free lag and rolling-mean features, calendar/oil-price/holiday signals, forecast-vs-actual plots for the biggest families, and feature importances showing recent rolling averages as the strongest predictors.
